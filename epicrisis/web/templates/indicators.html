Indicators

The same test is printed differently by every laboratory. An indicator puts those spellings under one label, so a history can be asked for once. Printed names, units and ranges never change. A model fills these groups where it is sure and marks them as not looked at yet: your part is to read them and take out what does not belong. Where it is unsure, it leaves a spelling waiting instead.
