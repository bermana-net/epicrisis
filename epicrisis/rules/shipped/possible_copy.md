# What it looks at

Documents of the same date, in different files, that have been transcribed. Two ways of being the
same result: the same named results (whatever the length — one file may be an excerpt or an export
of the other), or, for documents of similar length, mostly the same named results.

Names as well as numbers, always. Numbers alone made copies of two forms that had nothing to do
with each other: a urinalysis and a coprogram of one day print the same handful of small numbers —
0, 1, 1-2, 2-3 — and were filed as one document, after which one of them answered nothing at all,
because only one document of a group of copies is shown.


# How it can be wrong

Two genuinely different forms of one day, from one laboratory, ordering the same panel twice — a
morning and an evening draw — look exactly like a copy of each other and are not one. Nothing is
merged and no value is deleted: every document stays whole and the group is shown with the choice of
which file answers.

What is true, and was written here as though it were not, is that one document of a group answers
for it. The others are still there, and a question that does not ask for all_copies does not get
them: that is what keeps one blood draw, exported three times, from appearing three times in a
series. So switching this rule off is not only switching off a line in the list of work. It stops
the folding as well, and then the same measurement appears two or three times in every series, on
every chart, in every median and in every answer over the network, with nothing saying why. The
switch says that where it is pressed.
